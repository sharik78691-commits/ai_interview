It helps you prepare for technical, behavioral and coding interviews — bullet answers, STAR outlines, complexity notes and likely follow-ups.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Read our data practices or try the demo — no payment needed.

Will I be charged again?

No. Every plan is a one-time payment that simply ends. No auto-renewal and no stored cards.

Is my resume private?

Saved resumes and target roles are stored in your account. During a session, questions with your resume and job description go to the selected AI provider, and audio clips you share go to a speech-to-text provider. Transcripts and answers are not stored. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I try before paying?

Yes — the demo shows the full interview screen with sample answers, no payment needed. @if (hasTrial()) { Some plans also include a free trial you can start from Billing. } See the Refund Policy for payment questions.

How does the mock interview work?

Choose a role, interview type and difficulty. OyeInterview asks practice questions, you answer in your own words (typed or spoken), and you get a score out of 10 with what worked, what to improve and what a strong answer covers. Retry any question until it feels natural.

Do I need a microphone?

No. Practice questions come from the mock-interview or practice-call audio you share, and you can always type questions. Your own microphone is optional.

Which browsers work best?

Chrome or Edge — they support sharing the audio of a practice call from a browser tab. Typed questions and mock interviews work in other modern browsers.

Can I use it in a real interview?

Only where the interview's rules allow assistance tools. Check first — see our Acceptable Use Policy. AI suggestions can be wrong — always check them.
