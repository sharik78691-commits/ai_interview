What is OyeInterview?

OyeInterview is a real-time AI interview assistant that listens to your interview, detects the interviewer's questions, and instantly drafts structured answers tailored to your resume and the job description. Whether you are preparing for a technical interview, a behavioral interview, or a live coding interview, OyeInterview acts as your private interview copilot — surfacing bullet answers, STAR stories, complexity notes and follow-up questions the moment you need them.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a microphone?

No. You can type questions manually or run the one-click demo simulation. A microphone enables live transcription of the interviewer.

Which browsers work best?

Chrome or Edge — they support the Web Speech recognition API. Other browsers fall back to typing.

Where does my data go?

Resume text and job description are stored in your browser's localStorage and sent to the backend only to generate guidance.

Can I use it in a real interview?

Check the interview rules first. It's designed as a practice and preparation companion.

Does it write code for me?

It gives hints, complexity notes and skeletons — you still own the final answer.

Is OyeInterview free to try?

Yes. You can create a free account and run the built-in demo without any setup.
