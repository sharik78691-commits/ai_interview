Billing questions

Does any plan renew automatically?

No. Every purchase is a one-time payment for a fixed period. We don't save cards or set up recurring mandates.

What if I buy again before my period ends?

The new period is added after your current one ends, so you don't lose any days.

How do I pay?

Through our payment partners (Razorpay or Cashfree, whichever is enabled). Available methods such as UPI, cards and net banking are offered on the gateway's own checkout page.

When does my plan start?

As soon as the payment gateway confirms the payment to our server — usually within seconds. If confirmation is delayed, your plan activates automatically when it arrives.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds follow our Refund & Cancellation Policy. Please read it before purchasing.

Do prices include taxes?

The amount shown at checkout is the amount you pay. Tax treatment is described in our Terms.
