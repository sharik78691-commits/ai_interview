Billing questions

Does any plan renew automatically?

No. Passes are one-time payments. The Monthly and Quarterly plans are also paid upfront for 30 or 90 days — we don't save cards or set up recurring mandates.

When exactly does my access end?

Exactly the time shown after your payment is confirmed — not at midnight. A 1 Day Pass bought at 11:15 PM ends at 11:15 PM the next day. Your Billing page shows the end time in your local time zone.

What if I buy again before my plan ends?

The new purchase starts immediately with its own time, and nothing you already have is reset.

Which currency am I charged in?

The one shown at checkout. Prices in each currency are set by us directly — they are not converted live — and you are never charged in a different currency than the one you confirmed.

How do I pay?

Through our {{ partnerLabel() }}. Available methods such as UPI, cards and net banking are offered on the gateway's own checkout page.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds follow our Refund & Cancellation Policy. Please read it before purchasing.

Do prices include taxes?

The amount shown at checkout is the amount you pay. {{ gstNote }}
