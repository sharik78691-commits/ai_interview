Billing questions

How does auto-renewal work?

When your current period ends, the same plan renews automatically at the same price. We send a reminder before each renewal and you can cancel it anytime.

Can I cancel auto-renewal?

Yes. Go to your account settings and turn off auto-renewal. You keep access until the end of the current period — no partial-day charges.

What payment methods do you accept?

UPI, debit and credit cards, and net banking, processed securely by Razorpay and Cashfree. We never store your full card details.

Can I get a refund?

OyeInterview is a digital service delivered instantly, so refunds follow our Refund & Cancellation Policy. Please review it before purchasing.

Do prices include taxes?

Yes — listed prices include GST where applicable. The final amount shown at checkout is the amount you pay.
