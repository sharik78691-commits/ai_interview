Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

OyeInterview is built to help you prepare and communicate your own experience better. This policy sets out what is not allowed. It is part of our Terms of Service.

1. Follow the rules of every interview

  • Respect the rules of the interviewer, employer, recruiter, school, exam body or assessment platform. If a process forbids assistance tools, AI help or screen-capture software, do not use OyeInterview in it.
  • Do not use OyeInterview to misrepresent your identity, qualifications or experience.
  • Only capture or share audio and screens where you are allowed to, and comply with recording and consent laws that apply to you and to other participants.

2. Respect other people's data

  • Only upload resumes, job descriptions and other content that you have the right to use.
  • Do not upload other people's personal data without their permission, or sensitive data that the service does not need.

3. Do not misuse the service

  • No illegal, harassing, hateful, defamatory or fraudulent use, and no content that infringes others' rights.
  • No attempts to break, probe or overload the service: no bypassing authentication, rate limits or payment checks, no exploiting bugs, no automated scraping or bulk requests.
  • No sharing, renting or reselling accounts, and no creating multiple accounts to get repeated free trials.
  • No attempts to extract the underlying AI providers' models or to use the service to build a competing product.
  • No payment fraud, including chargebacks for purchases you made and used.

4. AI output

AI suggestions can be wrong. Do not present them as verified facts, and do not use them for decisions where an error could cause harm. You are responsible for what you say.

5. Enforcement

We may warn, limit, suspend or close accounts that break this policy, and may refuse refunds for access ended for that reason, as set out in the Refund Policy. Where we are legally required, we may report unlawful activity to the authorities.

6. Reporting

Report misuse or security issues to support@oyeinterview.com.
