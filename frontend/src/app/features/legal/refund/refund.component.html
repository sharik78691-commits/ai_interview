Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how cancellations, auto-renewal, and refunds work for OyeInterview subscriptions. Please read it before subscribing.

1. Nature of the service

OyeInterview is a digital service that is delivered and consumed immediately upon activation (real-time AI interview guidance). Because access is instant and usage cannot be returned, most purchases are non-refundable, subject to the limited exceptions below.

2. Cancellation of auto-renewal

You can cancel auto-renewal at any time:

  • From your account settings, or
  • By emailing support@oyeinterview.com.

Cancellation stops the next renewal. Your current period stays active until its end date — you are not billed again and there are no cancellation fees.

3. When we issue a refund

  • Duplicate charge: if you were charged twice for the same period, the duplicate is refunded in full.
  • Service not delivered: if payment succeeded but your plan was never activated due to a technical fault on our side, you get a full refund.
  • Unauthorized charge: if a charge was made without your authorisation, report it within 7 days for a full refund.

4. When we do NOT issue a refund

  • After the service has been accessed or used, since it is instantly consumed.
  • If you simply changed your mind after the plan was activated.
  • For partial periods when you cancel mid-cycle — you keep access until the period ends.
  • If access was terminated for a violation of our Terms and Conditions.

5. How to request a refund

Email support@oyeinterview.com with your registered email, order/transaction reference, and the reason. We review requests within 5 business days. Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 7–10 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

6. Failed or pending payments

If a payment fails or remains pending, no access is granted and any amount deducted is automatically reversed by the payment provider per its own policy. Contact support if a reversal does not appear within 7 days.

7. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email support@oyeinterview.com.
