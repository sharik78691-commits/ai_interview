Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

How cancellations and refunds work for OyeInterview's prepaid plans. Please read it before purchasing.

1. How plans work

Every plan is a one-time payment for a fixed number of days. There is no automatic renewal and nothing to cancel to avoid future charges — your plan simply ends on its expiry date. You can see your plan and its expiry on the Billing page.

2. Cancelling

Because no recurring charge exists, "cancelling" just means not buying another period. Access already purchased continues until its expiry date. Ending access early does not by itself create a refund.

3. When you are entitled to a refund

  • Duplicate payment: charged more than once for the same purchase — the extra payment is refunded in full.
  • Plan not activated: payment succeeded but your plan never became active because of a fault on our side, and we cannot fix it promptly — full refund.
  • Unauthorised payment: reported to us within 7 days — full refund after verification.
  • Service unavailable: if the service was unusable for a significant part of a period you bought because of a problem on our side, we offer extra days or a proportional refund.

4. When refunds are not normally given

  • A change of mind after the plan has been activated and used.
  • Unused days at the end of a period.
  • Accounts suspended for breaking the Terms or the Acceptable Use Policy.

This does not affect any rights you have under applicable consumer-protection law.

5. How to request a refund

Email support@oyeinterview.com from your registered email with the payment reference shown on your Billing page and the reason. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method through the payment gateway; the time to reach your account depends on your bank or payment provider.

6. Failed or pending payments

If a payment fails, no plan is activated. If money was deducted for a failed payment, the payment gateway or your bank normally reverses it automatically. A pending payment activates your plan automatically once the gateway confirms it. Contact support with the reference if a reversal or activation does not happen.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@oyeinterview.com.
