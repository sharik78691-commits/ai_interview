2. Audio and transcription

  • Your microphone: transcribed by your browser's built-in speech recognition. We never receive your microphone audio — only the resulting text. Note that some browsers (for example Google Chrome) perform this recognition on the browser vendor's servers under the vendor's own privacy terms.
  • Interviewer audio (meeting or browser tab you choose to share): short audio clips are sent to our server and passed to a speech-to-text provider to detect questions. We do not save these clips.

4. Desktop app

If you use the OyeInterview desktop app's screenshot analysis, only the screenshots you select are sent securely from your computer to our server, which forwards them to our AI provider (currently Google Gemini) to generate the analysis. You do not need to supply any API key. Screenshots are processed in memory and are not stored on our servers; we keep only a usage record (a request ID, the number of screenshots, the result status and the time) to count your plan's screenshot credits. The analysis history (text only, no images) is kept only on your device.

Do not select screenshots that show confidential information you are not allowed to share, or other people's personal data.

5. Cookies and browser storage

  • Strictly necessary cookies: a sign-in session cookie (HttpOnly), a security token that protects against cross-site request forgery, and a short-lived cookie used during Google sign-in.
  • Browser local storage: your theme and display preferences, and a copy of your current resume text and job description so the interview screen works quickly. Signing out clears the interview data from this browser.
  • We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, resumes and target roles: while your account exists. You can delete individual resumes and roles yourself at any time.
  • Payment and subscription records: kept after a plan ends, because they are financial records.
  • Activity events: retained to understand long-term service usage; we may delete or aggregate older records.
  • Server logs (request metadata such as time, path, status and technical errors): kept for troubleshooting and security.

7. Your choices and rights

  • View and edit your resumes and target roles from the dashboard, and see your subscription and payment history on the Billing page.
  • Request a copy, correction or deletion of your personal data, or deletion of your account, by contacting us (account deletion is handled by support today). Payment records may be retained where the law requires it.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and raise a complaint with the authority applicable to you. For any privacy concern you can also write to our grievance officer, {{ biz.operatorName }}, at {{ biz.email }}.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, Argon2id password hashing, HttpOnly session cookies with cross-site-request-forgery protection, rate limiting on sign-in, per-user access checks on every account record, and signature verification for payment notifications. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security; see our Trust page for details.
