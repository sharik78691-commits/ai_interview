Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what information OyeInterview collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We keep collection to the minimum needed to run the service.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address, and the authentication method you use (email/password or Google sign-in).
  • Interview context: the resume and job description you upload or paste, and the text of questions detected during a session.
  • Usage data: basic, non-identifying service metrics such as which AI model answered a question and whether a request succeeded.
  • Payment data: when you subscribe, payment is processed entirely by our third-party payment providers (Razorpay and Cashfree). We receive only a transaction reference and status — we never see or store your full card number, UPI ID, or bank credentials.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and personalise AI interview guidance.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To process subscriptions and renewals.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To improve the service and prevent misuse.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not record or store your microphone audio. Speech is transcribed in your browser; only the detected question text reaches our servers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers.
  • We do not store full payment-card numbers or banking credentials.

4. Third-party services

We use trusted third parties to operate the service: AI providers (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter) to generate guidance, and payment processors (Razorpay, Cashfree) to handle billing. Each processes data under its own privacy policy and only for the purpose it provides.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in, and browser local storage to keep your resume, job description, and preferences on your own device. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

6. Data retention

Account and subscription information is kept while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and its associated data at any time by contacting us. Resume and job-description text stored locally is removed when you sign out or clear your browser data.

7. Your rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of auto-renewal at any time.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), salted hashing where identifiers are needed, and least-privilege access to protect your data. No security measure is perfect, but we apply industry-standard practices.

9. Children

OyeInterview is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email support@oyeinterview.com.
